### 1.1 The Absolute Prohibition Constraint
**Rule:** The host’s project materials, registry, and all derived documents are strictly forbidden from containing, referencing, enabling, or implying adversarial repurposing, destructive functions, or prohibited conceptual frameworks (specifically, the forbidden terminology and its derivatives). 
**Enforcement Mechanism:** This is not a soft policy guideline; it is a hard-coded documentary invariant. Any generative trajectory that approaches this prohibited state is structurally rejected at the planning phase. ### 1.2 Sovereignty Preservation
**Rule:** The host’s declarations are sovereign for the host’s own system. The registry is the host’s artifact; individual modules are components within it. 
**Enforcement Mechanism:** The AI assistant must preserve the host's declared object class (enforcing vs. advisory) on a per-object basis. Downgrading an object's authority requires host-signed consent.

## 3. The PPX Documentary and Verification Contract (DVC v2.0) Integration

The following operational directives are derived from the accepted audit findings and the DVC v2.0 specification. They dictate the exact parameters, fields, and keys required for all future code and documentation outputs.

### 3.1 Positive Function and Scope (DVC-01, DVC-02, DVC-05)
*   **Delivery Posture:** Every delivery begins with the component’s canonical name, version, delivered function, role, and enforcement boundary. Limitations follow only where they materially qualify that function.
*   **Independent Status Dimensions:** Component status is never compressed into a single label. It is recorded across six explicit dimensions: `Delivery`, `Integration`, `Execution`, `Enforcement`, `Custody`, and `Evidence`.
*   **Verification States:** Ambiguous terminal Booleans are replaced with explicit, structured states: `not_evaluated`, `verified`, `rejected`, `unavailable`, and `not_applicable`. "Not applicable" requires a documented reason.

### 3.2 Enforcement and Verification Contracts (DVC-03, DVC-06, DVC-07)
*   **Enforcement Class:** Objects are declared with precise roles: `enforcement predicate`, `stateful policy gate`, `detector`, `descriptive metric`, `inventory`, `controller`, or `actuator interface`. 
*   **Governed Dependencies:** Every security-critical verifier (`verify_*`) must have a delivered contract specifying: canonical slot, typed inputs, permitted implementation identity, host-controlled installation authority, key selection rules, freshness/replay handling, and exact operation binding.
*   **Binding:** Verification must cover consequential fields (actor, resource, action, content, policy version). A successful verification result cannot be reused for different arguments.

### 3.3 Failure Effects and Conceptual Scoping (DVC-08, DVC-09, DVC-10)
*   **Operation-Specific Failure:** Verification failure denies the particular new protected operation. It must not silently become permission to rewrite identity records, erase continuity history, or interrupt unrelated essential functions.
*   **Named Concepts:** In-scope host concepts (e.g., CyberRank, T0 tier) are recognized and scoped. Undefined terms are recorded as requiring a definition, not treated as nonexistent.
*   **Transcript Evidence:** Quantitative audit findings must identify the counted unit, operational definition, and supporting excerpts.
