## Build summary

Use two linked review tasks. Source checking includes semantic judgment; the
separation concerns the question reviewed, not a claim of independent truth.

## Worksheet A: source fidelity

For each item, record state, evidence location, explanation, and needed check.

- Source identity and version match the citation.
- Relevant corrections, concerns, or withdrawals have been checked to the
  extent permitted and available; record coverage, not assumed completeness.
- Passage supports the recorded quantity and sign.
- Units, denominator, reference group, and effect measure are preserved.
- Observation unit, analysis unit, and target scope are not conflated.
- Observation period and location are preserved.
- Observation, estimate, interpretation, and projection are distinguished.
- Secondary reporting is not described as the original study.
- Limitations and uncertainty relevant to the claim are retained.

Overall source-fidelity status: reviewed_supported / reviewed_not_supported /
review_inconclusive. This is not a scientific-validity verdict.

## Worksheet B: scientific review

- What is the estimand?
- Does design support that estimand and intended application?
- What are the sampling, treatment, analysis, and inferential units?
- Are replication, dependence, shared controls, and repeated measures handled?
- Are detectability, censoring, missingness, and exclusions addressed?
- Are units, geometry, exchange, and measurement support appropriate?
- Which causal claims, if any, are justified?
- What alternative explanations remain?
- What model assumptions and diagnostics are available?
- What uncertainty is absent or inadequately quantified?
- Is extrapolation beyond the source justified?
- Does the proposed conclusion require a valid inferential bridge?

Overall scientific status: supported_within_scope / unsupported_for_use /
inconclusive. State scope and residual limitations explicitly.

## Reconciliation and release

Record differences between the two reviews. A faithful summary can be
scientifically unsupported; a valid study can be summarized incorrectly.
Do not require agreement by erasing disagreement.

Record review separately from local storage, indexing, synchronization, and
publication decisions. No checkbox in this worksheet authenticates authority.

## Workflow study

Compare combined review, sequential review, and independent sequential review
on prospectively selected packets. Separate training and evaluation packets.
Measure error detection, reviewer time, unresolved cases, false reassurance,
and disagreement. Randomize workflow assignment where feasible and account
for reviewer and study clustering. Do not treat multiple claims from one study
as automatically independent evidence.
